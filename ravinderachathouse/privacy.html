Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 Apps: Ravindera Chat House, Ravindera Merchant
This Privacy Policy explains how Ravindera Chat House (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile applications: Ravindera Chat House (customer app, package com.ravinderachathouse) and Ravindera Merchant (staff app, package com.ravinderachathouse.merchant), including related backend services hosted on Google Firebase.

1. Who we are

Ravindera Chat House is a food outlet in Nabha, Punjab, India. These apps let customers browse the menu, place dine-in or pickup orders for collection at the counter, and track order status. The merchant app is for authorised shop staff to manage orders and menu availability/prices.

Contact for privacy requests: gorisweet77@gmail.com (replace with your preferred shop support email before publishing if different).

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the apps, we may collect:

  • Account & identity: phone number (via Firebase Phone Authentication), and the display name you provide in your profile.
  • Order data: items ordered, packing options, customizations, order type (dine-in / pickup), amounts (stored in paise), order status history, and related timestamps.
  • Device & push tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration tokens so we can send order-related notifications. On Android 13+, notification permission is requested.
  • App / technical logs: limited diagnostics that Firebase or the device OS may generate (for example crash or delivery errors). We do not sell advertising profiles.
  • Staff access data (merchant app only): authenticated staff accounts with an admin privilege used to update order status and catalog fields.

Guests may browse the menu without signing in. Checkout, orders, profile, and notifications require phone sign-in.

3. How we use information

  • Create and authenticate your account
  • Place, validate, and fulfill orders at the counter
  • Show live order status and order history
  • Send push notifications about order updates (customers) and new orders (staff)
  • Let authorised staff update order status and menu price/availability
  • Operate, secure, and troubleshoot the apps and Firebase backends
  • Comply with legal obligations if required

Payment in the current version is pay at counter only. We do not collect card or UPI credentials through these apps.

4. Legal bases (where applicable)

We process information to provide the service you request (orders and account), with your consent where required (for example notification permission), and for our legitimate interests in running a secure food-ordering service for our shop.

5. Sharing of information

We share data only as needed to run the service:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (processing on our behalf).
  • Shop staff — order details needed to prepare and hand over your order.
  • Legal / safety — if required by law or to protect rights, safety, or the integrity of the service.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Data retention

We keep account, order, and related records for as long as needed to operate the shop apps, resolve disputes, and meet legal or accounting needs. Push tokens are updated or removed when they become invalid or when you sign out / uninstall (subject to device and Firebase behaviour). You may request deletion of your account data using the contact email above; we will delete or anonymise personal data unless we must retain it for legitimate business or legal reasons (for example completed order records).

7. Security

We use Firebase Authentication, security rules, and server-side order placement/status updates to limit unauthorised access. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please protect your device and do not share OTP codes.

8. Children’s privacy

The apps are intended for general audiences placing food orders. They are not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

9. Your choices & rights

  • Decline or revoke notification permission in system settings
  • Sign out of the apps
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by emailing us
  • Uninstall the apps to stop further local collection on that device

10. International processing

Our backends use Google Firebase / Google Cloud, which may process data in regions selected for the project (including India) and other Google infrastructure locations as described in Google’s documentation. By using the apps, you understand your information may be processed in these locations.
