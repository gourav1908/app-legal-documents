Delete your account

App: Ravindera Chat House Package: com.ravinderachathouse Effective date: 11 November 2026
This page explains how to request deletion of your Ravindera Chat House customer account and associated personal data. Use these steps if you created an account by signing in with your phone number (OTP).

How to request account deletion

  1. Email gorisweet77@gmail.com from an email address we can use to reply to you.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my Ravindera Chat House account.
  3. In the message body, include:
    • The phone number registered in the app (with country code, e.g. +91…)
    • A short confirmation that you want the account permanently deleted
  4. We will verify the request (we may ask you to confirm ownership of the phone number) and process it within 30 days.

There is currently no in-app “Delete account” button. Email is the supported method. After deletion, sign-out / uninstall the app on your devices.

Data that is deleted

When your deletion request is completed, we delete or permanently de-identify:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account linked to that phone number
  • Your customer profile (including display name stored for the account)
  • Push notification (FCM) tokens stored for your user account
  • Other personal identifiers tied directly to your user ID that are not required for legal retention

Data that may be retained

Some order records may be kept for shop operations, dispute handling, accounting, or legal compliance (for example order number, items, amounts, and timestamps). Where possible we remove or anonymise direct identifiers (such as phone number and name) from retained records.

Any retained order-related records are kept only as long as needed for those purposes, and generally no longer than 24 months, unless a longer period is required by law.

Additional notes

  • Guest browsing does not create an account; there is nothing to delete for guests.
  • Denying notification permission or uninstalling the app does not by itself delete your account.
  • More detail on how we handle data is in our Privacy Policy.
